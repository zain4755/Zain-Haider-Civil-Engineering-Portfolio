# Zain Haider | Civil Engineering Portfolio

Civil engineering undergraduate with experience in structural design, physical modelling, laboratory testing, site analysis and technical reporting. This portfolio presents selected academic work and the engineering decisions behind each project.

## Selected projects

| Project | Focus | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Timber Truss: Design, Build and Test](projects/timber-truss/) | Structural analysis, material selection, cost optimisation and laboratory testing | The model carried the design load and failed at approximately 5.25 kN at the predicted glued joint. |
| [St. George's Field Pavilion](projects/st-georges-field-pavilion/) | Concept development, site response, physical modelling and construction planning | Initial concepts, final design board, details and material-cost estimate. |
| [Vertical Barrier](projects/vertical-barrier/) | Concept evaluation, recycled materials, dimensions, cost analysis and constructability | Comparison matrix and developed final scheme using container cladding and a steel frame. |
| [St. George's Field Site Analysis](projects/site-analysis/) | Access, topography, drainage, ecology, heritage and stakeholder analysis | Consolidated mapping, observations and hand-drawn site studies. |

## Engineering strengths demonstrated

- Structural calculations and comparison of predicted and experimental behaviour
- Load-deflection data collection, interpretation and presentation
- Material selection, cost estimation and constructability thinking
- Technical drawing, 3D visualisation and physical model development
- Site appraisal covering access, geology, topography, drainage, ecology and stakeholders
- Clear technical reporting and collaborative design communication

## About the work

The timber-truss report documents an academic design-build-test exercise. The pavilion, vertical-barrier and site-analysis submissions were completed as group projects. Group artefacts are included to demonstrate collaborative engineering work and do not imply sole authorship.

Public copies have been prepared without student numbers or classmates' full personal details.
